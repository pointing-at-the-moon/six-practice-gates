# The Six Practice Gates
## 六行門——行深般若波羅蜜多的六扇門

*A bilingual project in the studies wing of [Waken / Pointing at the Moon](https://github.com/pointing-at-the-moon). Its papers are descriptive historical and doctrinal studies of six Buddhist practice gates; its stepped-down book layers are pastoral continuous readings and may include practice-facing guidance under each volume's own style.*

六行門屬於 Waken 的**研究翼（studies wing）**。論文層以描述性、教育性的方式，考察念、施、懺、戒、定、願六種行門的歷史與義理；降階成書層則是法師對學人的連續開示，依各卷書寫規範可包含實踐對接。Waken 的中心仍是佛典的譯、疏、論。

The Heart Sūtra opens: *"When Avalokiteśvara was **practicing** the deep Prajñāpāramitā…"* This series studies six forms that Buddhist practice has taken and reads them, constructively, as one sequence ending in vow. That correspondence is the series' own hermeneutic—not an explicit statement of the Heart Sūtra and not a consensus of its commentarial traditions.

**念 → 施 → 懺 → 戒 → 定 → 願**

### The series' constructive reading / 六門的書系讀法

The closing paper presents the series' own reading of the six gates as one deepening sequence alongside the Heart Sūtra—five movements and one emptiness:

> 觀 ／ 度 ／ **空** ／ 不 ／ 究竟 ／ 得菩提
> 念 ／ 施 ／ **懺** ／ 戒 ／ 定 ／ 願

懺 is the one *subtractive* gate (the Heart Sūtra's emptiness, 罪性本空); 願 alone unseals 得菩提 — the vow that turns the bodhisattva's ultimate nirvana outward into the Buddhas' full awakening. The sequence closes here, handed back to view (the Octalogy) and to the three trainings.

> **Attribution boundary:** 觀／度／空／不／究竟／得菩提 ↔ 念／施／懺／戒／定／願 is an authorial architecture used by this series. The scriptural phrases are canonical; the sixfold correspondence is not presented as the Heart Sūtra's own stated sequence.

### How to read / 怎麼讀

Every paper is bilingual — 中文 and English side by side. The six volumes can be read in order, or entered through any single gate: walking through one naturally engages the others. Per-paper detail lives in each volume's README.

Beyond the papers, each gate has a **book layer** (成書層) — the volume stepped down into continuous reading, 中文 gold standard with an English mirror:

### Canonical provenance / 經典來源契約

Every paper carries a `canonical_source` list in its frontmatter. Each T/X value has been verified against the CBETA Taishō/Xuzangjing XML editions (work number and extant witness both confirmed); Pāli values are enrolled only against publication-safe witnesses. Ambiguous work-family references are not guessed into a single witness.

This property records the canonical witnesses actually cited by the paper; it is not, by itself, a claim that every translation, inference, or authorial synthesis has received human ratification. Raw canonical text outranks catalogs, workflow notes, and prior reports. The book layers inherit the papers' citations and must label constructive or house-level readings rather than presenting them as words of a sūtra or śāstra.

每篇論文的 frontmatter 均列 `canonical_source`。T／X 編號均經 CBETA《大正藏》／《卍續藏》XML 原典逐一核驗（經號與實存見證並確認）；巴利來源僅在可公開之見證下納入。此欄記錄實際援引的原典見證，不等同人工審定，也不把作者綜合判作經論原說。
